Many of Vietnam’s heritage sites, craft villages and old houses hold wonderful stories, yet the information about them online is thin, scattered and often inaccurate. **Ke Mien Di San** ("Telling the Heritage of the Regions") exists to change that.

## What is Ke Mien Di San?

Ke Mien Di San is a community brand developed and run by Nam Kinh Digital for local people, travellers and anyone who loves culture. It brings together a website, a Facebook Page and a community of content contributors.

## What can you do there?

- Explore places, view 360° imagery and maps.
- Read the cultural and historical stories of each region.
- Contribute photos and material you have the right to share.
- Add or correct place information and vote for the best content.

Contributors are credited and can take part in community reward programmes.

## How is content reviewed?

Everything the community submits is reviewed, standardized and rights-managed by Nam Kinh Digital before it is published. We respect authorship and the accuracy of heritage.

## Connecting to destinations

When a destination is featured on Ke Mien Di San, more people get to know its story. Over time, that content can link to experiences bookable on Nam Kinh Digital’s platform, taking travellers from "heard of it" to "visited it".
