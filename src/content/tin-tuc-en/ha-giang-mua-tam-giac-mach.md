From late October to mid-November, buckwheat flowers blanket the rocky plateau. Here is a suggested itinerary for first-time visitors.

## Day 1: Hanoi – Ha Giang

Leave early in the morning and reach Ha Giang city in the afternoon. Visit Kilometre Zero and rest.

## Day 2: Quan Ba – Yen Minh – Dong Van

Stop at Quan Ba Heaven Gate and the Yen Minh pine forest. Spend the night at a homestay in Dong Van old quarter.

## Day 3: Ma Pi Leng – Nho Que

Take a boat along the Nho Que River and admire the Tu San canyon. In the afternoon, visit the buckwheat fields.

## Day 4: Return

Stop by a local market if the day matches, then head back to Hanoi.
